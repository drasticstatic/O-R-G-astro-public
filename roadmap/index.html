🧭 Roadmap

TL;DR: This lab is Christopher's changelog and portfolio of what he builds for ORG. Next is helping ORG decide how the new builds come together with orgspirituality.org, and making the dApp preview's crypto giving real once ORG designates an address. No dates.

Where the drasticstatic ORG lab is headed, in rough order. What’s done is in the lab changelog; ORG’s own plans are on the ORG AstroLog’s roadmap. This is also the repository’s ROADMAP.md.

✅ Just landed

  • ORG’s kit 1.4 in the lab: Donate and the welcome guide in every footer, More menu and Menu, and the Community window with all four of ORG’s Telegram chats (Tripp OK’d): the public chat, events and ceremonies, and the members’ two private groups.
  • ORG’s kit 1.2 in the lab: one navbar on every page that folds into a Menu, footer link groups, Reach out and Community windows (ORG’s), and this roadmap and a sustainability page.
  • The map with pictures, drawn from ORG’s copy: every destination as a card, then the diagram.

🔜 Next

  • The new builds and orgspirituality.org, together. The open question is how this lab’s work comes together with ORG’s official site. The likely direction is a new, web3-ready site. Help decide.
  • Crypto giving, made real. The dApp preview’s gift page runs on a placeholder address until ORG designates and verifies a real one. When it does, the same wallet connection can fill the “give with crypto” slot on ORG’s donate page.

🤝 Stewardship and succession

So this work outlives any one person (today drasticstatic, Christopher W, holds every key to the lab and the dApp preview). Details on the sustainability page.

  • A home in ORG’s organization for what ORG wants to keep, with at least two owners from ORG’s own people
  • Publishing through org-owned automation (a GitHub App, or an org bot account), not personal tokens
  • A token register and a renewal runbook, with a reminder a month before each expiry
  • Hand-over steps written down: transferring these repositories, the Pages sites and the wallet-connect project
  • Exports and backups of the repositories, on a schedule
  • Wallet connect: ORG’s own Reown project (a one-line org.js change, give.reownProjectId; the dApp’s secret follows), before any treasury address is published

🌌 Later, and still being explored

  • A network of ORG’s own. A small social network ORG’s members own, on open protocols like Lens and Farcaster, paired with a DAO where members propose and vote. A direction, not a promise.
