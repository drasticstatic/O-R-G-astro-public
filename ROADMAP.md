Where the drasticstatic ORG lab is headed, in rough order. What's done is in the
[lab changelog](https://drasticstatic.github.io/O-R-G-astro-public/changelog); ORG's own plans are
on the [ORG AstroLog's roadmap](https://octagon-religious-research-group-org.github.io/changelog-astro-public/roadmap).
This is also the repository's `ROADMAP.md`.

## ✅ Just landed

- **ORG's kit 1.2 in the lab:** one navbar on every page that folds into a Menu, footer link groups,
  Reach out and Community windows (ORG's), and this roadmap and a sustainability page.
- **The map with pictures**, drawn from ORG's copy: every destination as a card, then the diagram.

## 🔜 Next

- **The new builds and orgspirituality.org, together.** The open question is how this lab's work
  comes together with ORG's official site. The likely direction is a new, web3-ready site.
  [Help decide](https://octagon-religious-research-group-org.github.io/changelog-astro-public/three-directions#what-comes-next).
- **Crypto giving, made real.** The [dApp preview](https://drasticstatic.github.io/O-R-G-dapp-public-preview/)'s
  gift page runs on a placeholder address until ORG designates and verifies a real one. When it
  does, the same wallet connection can fill the "give with crypto" slot on
  [ORG's donate page](https://octagon-religious-research-group-org.github.io/donate/).

## 🌌 Later, and still being explored

- **A network of ORG's own.** A small social network ORG's members own, on open protocols like Lens
  and Farcaster, paired with a DAO where members propose and vote. A direction, not a promise.
