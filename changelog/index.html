Changelog

What changed in the lab and its previews, newest first. Each entry names who made it.

ORG's official changelog is the ORG AstroLog. It's the record for every repository in ORG's own GitHub organization.

Entries marked sidecar era date from before this site became the lab, when it also tracked ORG's own sites. They stay here as history.

October 2026

  1. Oct 13Lab

    One navbar, a map with pictures, and every way into ORG

    The lab now runs on ORG's kit 1.2. Every page has one navbar that never wraps (Lab, Changelog, Ledger, Map, the dApp preview, an ORG menu, Get involved and About, an icon on each), folding into a Menu button on smaller screens, with the same links in groups at the foot of each page. Reach out and Community open ORG's own windows: its addresses, Tripp, its CONTACTS.md, and its Telegram group. The map now shows every destination as a card with the picture its link shows, the lab and the dApp preview included, above the diagram, all drawn from ORG's one copy. New Roadmap and Sustainability pages say where the lab is headed and what keeps it running. Names read first name and last initial. See it

    Christopher W

  2. Oct 12Preview

    The dApp preview joins the suite

    The dApp preview now runs on ORG's arrival kit, like this lab and ORG's own sites: the first-visit welcome, the light & motion controls, reading mode, contrast, cursor effects, the welcome guide, / to search, and a count of what's new here since your last visit. A strip at the top links straight to this changelog. The "three directions" showcase moved off its home page and into ORG's AstroLog, where Renan's proposal is kept as history; everything else about the dApp works as before. See it

    drasticstatic with Alfred

  3. Oct 12Lab

    A ledger of every public change, in plain words

    A new Ledger page lists every public change to the lab and the dApp preview: tidied into a plain sentence, sorted by kind (look and feel, records, new things, behind the scenes…), grouped by day and site, and linked to the change itself. It's gathered from the public mirrors only and rebuilt every day, and the same daily rebuild redraws the map from ORG's latest copy. ORG's own repositories have their ledger in the ORG AstroLog. See it

    drasticstatic with Alfred

  4. Oct 12Lab

    Reading mode, and what's new since your last visit

    The book button in the header is reading mode: one tap for Stone & Lapis, still ink, more contrast and no cursor trail, and another tap brings back what you had. Coming back later, a small count beside the search button shows lab entries and ledger changes that are new since your last visit, and opening it clears it.

    drasticstatic with Alfred
